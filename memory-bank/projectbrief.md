# Project Brief: Dani's Kitchen

## Overview
Dani's Kitchen is a website for a mobile food truck, primarily serving coffee, fresh pastries (croissants, banana bread, cookies), and toasties. The site transitioned from an AI-generated taco truck template to a cozy, warm, and inviting aesthetic tailored to a talented chef whose dream has been to run her own food truck.

## Key Goals
- **Rebranding:** Shift the edgy, dark taco truck theme into a warm, inviting coffee and bakery vibe.
- **Menu Display:** Present the menu items (Food, Milk Options, Coffee) cleanly with placeholder prices.
- **Storytelling:** Highlight Dani's passion as a chef and baker.
- **Catering Requests:** Allow users to submit booking requests, with specific options for private events.
- **Modularity:** Ensure sections like Location can be easily toggled on or off as needed.

## Target Audience
Local coffee lovers, foodies, event planners looking for private catering, and anyone seeking high-quality, fresh-baked goods and coffee.
